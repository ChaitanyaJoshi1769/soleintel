## Quick Reference

**What to Include in Every Report:**
- ✅ Status emoji (green/yellow/red)
- ✅ Key metrics vs. targets
- ✅ Issues and resolutions
- ✅ Team sentiment
- ✅ Recognition and gratitude
- ✅ Tomorrow's focus

**What to Keep Out:**
- ❌ Over-detailed technical logs
- ❌ Complaining without solutions
- ❌ Vague language (use specifics)
- ❌ Negativity without acknowledgment of good work
- ❌ Unnecessary jargon

**Tone:**
- Professional but human
- Honest about challenges
- Celebrate wins
- Focus on forward momentum
- Recognize team effort
