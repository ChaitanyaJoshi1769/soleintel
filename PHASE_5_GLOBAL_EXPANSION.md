## Risk Assessment

### High-Risk Items

1. **Language/Localization Issues**
   - Risk: Poor translations reduce user satisfaction
   - Mitigation: Native speaker review, continuous improvement

2. **International Regulatory Compliance**
   - Risk: GDPR, data privacy violations
   - Impact: Legal issues, market exclusion
   - Mitigation: Legal review, compliance team

3. **Currency/Payment Issues**
   - Risk: Payment failures in new markets
   - Impact: Revenue loss
   - Mitigation: Multiple payment providers, local support

4. **Competitive Entry**
   - Risk: Established competitors copy features
   - Impact: Market share erosion
   - Mitigation: Rapid innovation, community lock-in

5. **Over-expansion**
   - Risk: Too many markets simultaneously
   - Impact: Resource strain, quality drop
   - Mitigation: Phased rollout, strong team
