## Messaging Guidelines

### Tone & Voice
- ✅ Professional but human
- ✅ Honest and transparent
- ✅ Data-driven
- ✅ Optimistic but realistic
- ✅ Team-focused
- ✅ User-centric

### What to Emphasize
- ✅ Strong metrics and evidence
- ✅ Team execution and capability
- ✅ User satisfaction and retention
- ✅ Market opportunity
- ✅ Growth trajectory
- ✅ Technical excellence

### What to Acknowledge
- ✅ Challenges and risks
- ✅ Areas for improvement
- ✅ Competition
- ✅ Market uncertainties
- ✅ Our learning process

### What to Avoid
- ❌ Over-promising
- ❌ Hiding bad news
- ❌ Vague metrics
- ❌ Defensive language
- ❌ Excuses
- ❌ Underselling achievements

## Distribution Checklist

**Before sending any external communication:**

- [ ] Review for accuracy (have all metrics been verified?)
- [ ] Check tone (professional, honest, optimistic?)
- [ ] Verify language (no jargon, clear to all audiences?)
- [ ] Include context (what do they need to understand this?)
- [ ] Add calls to action (what's next? what do you need?)
- [ ] Proofread (spelling, grammar, formatting)
- [ ] Route to PM/CEO (get approval before sending)
- [ ] Time zones (sending at appropriate time?)
- [ ] Follow-up plan (what's the next communication?)
