# Monthly Retrospective Template
## SOLEINTEL Continuous Learning & Improvement
**Template Created:** May 13, 2026  
**Used Starting:** June 2026 (ongoing monthly)  
**Purpose:** Systematic team learning and continuous improvement

---

## Overview

This template guides monthly retrospectives (last Friday of each month, 2pm PT). The goal is to:

✅ Celebrate wins  
✅ Identify what could improve  
✅ Extract learnings  
✅ Make concrete improvements  
✅ Strengthen team culture  

**Monthly Cadence:**
- Last Friday of month
- 90 minutes
- Full team + async input from those who can't attend
- Led by PM or team lead
- Documented and shared

## Meeting Agenda (90 minutes)

### Opening (5 min)

**Facilitator opens:**

```
"Hi everyone! This is our monthly retrospective for [Month].

The goal is to celebrate what we've done, learn from challenges, 
and improve for next month.

This is a safe space - no judgment, no blame. Just honest feedback 
to help us get better as a team.

Let's start with wins."
```

**Discussion:**

"Let's talk about what worked really well this month.

- [Theme 1]: [Example quote]
- [Theme 2]: [Example quote]
- [Theme 3]: [Example quote]

[Allow 10 min of open discussion]

- [Reason 1]
- [Reason 2]
- [Reason 3]

### What Needs Improvement (20 min)

**Discussion:**

"Now let's talk about challenges and frustrations.

- [Challenge 1]: [# people mentioned]

- [Challenge 3]: [# people mentioned]

[Repeat for each challenge]

[Allow 5 min for additional concerns]"

## Key Principles

✅ **Psychological Safety** - Be honest without fear  
✅ **Blamelessness** - Focus on systems, not individuals  
✅ **Action-Oriented** - Convert feedback to improvements  
✅ **Transparent** - Share all results with team  
✅ **Appreciative** - Celebrate before criticizing  
✅ **Forward-Looking** - Learn from past, focus on future  
✅ **Inclusive** - All voices heard, all perspectives valued  

---

## Facilitator Tips

**Great facilitators:**
- ✅ Start on time, end on time
- ✅ Keep discussion focused
- ✅ Draw out quiet voices ("What do you think, [Name]?")
- ✅ Validate all feedback ("That's important...")
- ✅ Convert feedback to action ("So the action would be...")
- ✅ Celebrate, then improve (always celebrate first)
- ✅ Keep it blameless (focus on systems)
- ✅ Follow up on action items (don't forget them)

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Going too long (respect people's time)
- ❌ Blaming individuals
- ❌ Dismissing feedback
- ❌ Not converting to action
- ❌ Forgetting action items
- ❌ Making it only about negatives
- ❌ Dominating the discussion
