## 🚀 Launch Day Success Targets (May 20)

### Primary Success Metrics

#### 1. App Store Availability
**Target:** Both iOS and Android apps available on their respective stores by 10:00am PT

**What This Means:**
- iOS app available in App Store
- Android app available in Google Play Store
- Apps visible in search results
- App pages fully functional (ratings, reviews, screenshots)

**Success Criteria:**
- ✅ Both apps downloadable by 10:00am
- ✅ Download links working
- ✅ App pages showing correctly
- ✅ Version shows as production version

**How We Track:**
- Manual verification by QA at 10:00am
- Sentry showing app launches
- Firebase showing user sessions

---

#### 2. Installation Rate
**Target:** 500+ installations by end of May 20

**What This Means:**
- Real users downloading from app stores
- Not company-internal downloads
- Not re-downloads from same users

**Success Criteria:**
- ✅ >500 downloads by 10pm
- ✅ >100 installations in first hour (10am-11am)
- ✅ >30 installations per hour average
- ✅ No sudden drops in installation rate

**How We Track:**
- Firebase analytics - new user count
- App store analytics
- Sentry - new app instances
- Manual tracking every hour

**Milestones:**
- 🟢 100 downloads by 11am → on track
- 🟡 50 downloads by 11am → slower than expected but OK
- 🔴 <10 downloads by 11am → major issue, check marketing

---

#### 3. Crash Rate
**Target:** <2% crash rate on launch day

**What This Means:**
- <2 crashes per 100 app sessions
- Users can complete their workflows without crashes
- No blocking issues

**Success Criteria:**
- ✅ Crash rate <2% overall
- ✅ No single issue causes >10% of crashes
- ✅ No app hangs or freezes
- ✅ No data loss reports

**How We Track:**
- Sentry - real-time crash tracking
- Firebase - crash analytics
- Manual testing from QA
- User support tickets

**Thresholds:**
- 🟢 <1% crash rate → excellent
- 🟡 1-2% crash rate → acceptable, monitor
- 🔴 2-5% crash rate → concerning, investigate
- 🚨 >5% crash rate → critical, consider rollback

---

#### 4. App Store Rating
**Target:** 4.0+ rating by May 27 (based on launch day reviews)

**What This Means:**
- Average rating across all user reviews
- Measured in app store (not internal)
- Reflects user satisfaction

**Success Criteria:**
- ✅ 4.0+ average rating
- ✅ >80% 4-5 star reviews
- ✅ Positive comments outnumber negative
- ✅ <10% 1-star reviews

**How We Track:**
- App Store review monitoring
- Firebase user feedback
- Support ticket sentiment

**Milestones:**
- 🟢 4.5+ rating → exceptional
- 🟢 4.0-4.5 rating → strong
- 🟡 3.5-4.0 rating → acceptable, work on improvements
- 🔴 <3.5 rating → significant issues to address

---

### Secondary Metrics (May 20)

#### Daily Active Users (DAU)
**Target:** 300+ DAU on May 20

**Success Criteria:**
- ✅ >300 users with at least 1 session
- ✅ Average session length >5 minutes
- ✅ >50% returning users by end of day

---

#### User Retention (Day 1)
**Target:** 50%+ of downloaders install and open app

**Success Criteria:**
- ✅ ≥50% of installers open app
- ✅ ≥30% complete full onboarding
- ✅ ≥10% complete first key action (browse, search, etc.)

---

#### API Response Time
**Target:** <500ms p99 latency all day

**Success Criteria:**
- ✅ Average latency <200ms
- ✅ p95 latency <400ms
- ✅ p99 latency <500ms
- ✅ No requests exceeding 2 seconds

**Thresholds:**
- 🟢 <300ms average → excellent
- 🟡 300-500ms average → acceptable, monitor
- 🔴 >500ms average → issue, investigate

## Contingency: If We Miss Targets

### If We're Below Target Downloads
**Action Plan:**
1. Analyze: Why are downloads slower?
   - Marketing reach issues
   - Product issues
   - Market timing
   - Competition

2. Accelerate:
   - Increase marketing spend
   - Reach out to influencers
   - Product improvements
   - Content marketing

3. Pivot:
   - Different positioning
   - Different target market
   - Feature adjustments
   - Pricing changes

### If App Store Rating Is Below 3.8
**Action Plan:**
1. Analyze: What's causing low ratings?
   - Read user reviews carefully
   - Check Sentry for crashes
   - Test user flows
   - Gather feedback

2. Fix:
   - Rapid iteration on issues
   - Address top complaints
   - Improve onboarding
   - Hotfixes for crashes

3. Reposition:
   - Reach out to 1-star reviewers
   - Ask for rating updates
   - Improve visibility of 5-star reviews
   - Continue improving product

### If Crash Rate Is Above 2%
**Action Plan:**
1. Immediate: Identify and fix critical issue
2. Quick: Deploy hotfix
3. Monitor: Verify crash rate improves
4. Analyze: Root cause analysis
5. Prevent: Additional testing/monitoring for next release
